# Policy mapping

**Primary authority:** SETU *Academic Unit Reviews Policy*, version 1.0, approved 23 September 2025.

This table restates the developer specification’s authority map (§2). The right-hand column is how
this prototype responds. It is a design proposal, **not** something the policy mandates. Policy
summaries are short paraphrases; consult the policy itself.

| Policy anchor | Requirement (summary) | Tool implementation (proposal) |
|---|---|---|
| §1.1, p.4 | Five-year cycle; faculty/department/sub-unit reviews; strategic direction, teaching/assessment, research, programme provision and third mission | Nine guided review areas; review-period and unit-type metadata |
| §2.1, p.5 | Consider NFQ/award standards, assessment, learner achievement, access, transfer and progression | Specific prompts and evidence suggestions (TLA, SES, PPA areas) |
| §4.1–4.2, pp.6–7 | Broad review of all academic activity, third-party arrangements and improvement | Cross-cutting question bank including PPA-05 (third-party arrangements, “where applicable”) |

## Deliberately not invented

The tool does not supply, and must not be read as supplying:

- the approved AUR Procedure (listed as pending in policy §6, p.10);
- official SER headings or template;
- fixed word counts, evidence minimums or rating scales;
- a compulsory nine-section structure (the nine areas and 45 questions are suggestions);
- prescribed consultation methods;
- deadlines.

## Decisions requiring institutional validation (specification §14)

1. Obtain the current approved AUR Procedure and official SER template from the Office of the Vice
   President for Academic Affairs, Teaching and Learning.
2. Agree whether the nine areas should be reorganised to match the official SER structure.
3. Confirm policy owner approval for reproducing policy excerpts and whether the tool may be published
   with SETU branding.
4. Confirm institutional data classification and whether any real review data may be used in a
   browser-local prototype.
5. Pilot with a representative academic unit and quality assurance colleagues.
